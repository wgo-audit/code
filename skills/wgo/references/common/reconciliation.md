# Reconciliation

Reconcile inside every lens and again in synthesis. Compare material evidence
and artifacts directly; never treat a summary as proof.

When sources conflict, state the exact tension, what each source establishes,
the risk of a wrong assumption, and the closure route. Add an open item only if
owner, authority, or proof is needed. Do not create a separate contradiction
register unless a material case genuinely needs its own artifact.

State when no material contradiction was found. Preserve post-cutoff and
inaccessible evidence limits in the affected lens report and final report.
