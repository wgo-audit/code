# Reviewer Report Template

Replace prompts in the completed report. Keep the template in English and
translate the generated report at synthesis when requested.

```markdown
# <Reviewer Name>

## Audit Question, Depth, And Evidence Boundary

State the audit question, detailed standard, cutoff, material evidence sources used, and exclusions.

## Coverage And Material Gaps

State what was examined and only material gaps. Link an open item or source-access event when a future owner, authority, or proof is needed.

## Key Findings

| Finding | Evidence links | Confidence and limitation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|

## Mandate-Relevant Strengths

Include only evidence-supported strengths that reduce a stated mandate concern.
Do not treat an unexamined area or missing evidence as a strength.

### Decision Insights

Include only when one or more evidence-linked findings change a decision,
priority, sequence, claim, or stop condition. For each, state the decision,
causal relationship or conflict, consequence, and smallest next proof/action.
Do not use a target count.

## Selected Outputs

Link each required or triggered output. Explain a required/triggered omission once, with evidence gap and closure route.

## Material Omissions, Unknowns, And Auditor Questions

State only questions that could change the result and their current route.
For a question raised to the auditor, record an answer and its effect only when
the answer is already available; otherwise state its route. Put proof-only needs
under verification rather than presenting them as questions.

## Reconciliation

State conflicts, changed evidence, and any prior-item disposition on a resumed
audit, or that no material reconciliation issue was found.

## Bounded Conclusion And Downstream Guidance

State what the reviewer establishes, what it does not establish, which reviewer
should use it next, and what that reviewer must not assume.
```
